How to Play:

  • Rotate View: Click and drag to rotate the pyramid
  • Zoom: Scroll to zoom in/out
  • Select Piece: Click a piece from the panel on the right
  • Place Piece: Click on a pyramid position to place (hover shows preview)
  • Rotate Piece: Use X, Y, Z buttons to rotate around each axis
  • Flip Piece: Use the Flip button to mirror the piece
  • Undo: Remove the last placed piece
  • Clear: Start over with an empty pyramid

Solving Options:

Use "Solve Pyramid" to find all solutions for your current configuration, or "Solve Empty" to solve from scratch.

• Time limit: Sets the per-request search time. Enter 0 to remove the limit. If the limit is reached, partial results are returned and you can continue loading by scrolling.
• Batch size: Controls how many solutions load per scroll. Defaults to 100. You can change it anytime; the next batch will use your new selection.

Quick Control Panel

Shortcuts: R rotate, F flip, Backspace undo, C clear. Set X/Y/Z then “Place at (X,Y,Z)” for precise placement.
